Webcam hand mouse · macOS

Your hand is the mouse.

Kinematic reads 21 hand landmarks from your webcam and turns them into pointer, click, drag and scroll. The cursor follows your palm, not a fingertip, so curling a finger never moves it.

Gesture map

Four poses.
One meaning each.

Poses resolve in a fixed priority: scroll first, then left, then right. Thresholds scale with your hand, so a pinch registers the same near the camera as far from it. Hover a row to see the pose in the preview.

  1. 01

    Thumb + index

    Press and hold the left button. Keep pinching to drag; open to release.

    Left click
  2. 02

    Thumb + middle

    Press and hold the right button, with the same hold time and cooldown.

    Right click
  3. 03

    Thumb on both

    Move the hand to scroll on both axes. This pose never clicks.

    Scroll
  4. 04

    Open hand, held

    Opt in with --gesture-pause. Hold the open hand for 1.2 s to pause or resume.

Under the hood

Hardware at the edges.
Pure logic inside.

The gesture logic takes timestamps as arguments and never sees a camera or a mouse. That is why the whole suite runs without a webcam, a display or a single real click.

  1. 01 · capture

    Threaded camera

    A background grabber hands over the newest frame, mirrored.

  2. 02 · landmarks

    MediaPipe Hands

    21 points, converted once at the boundary into plain dataclasses.

  3. 03 · smoothing

    One Euro filter

    A 4 Hz low-pass on the palm. No grid, no snapping.

  4. 04 · recognise

    State machine

    Hysteresis, hold time, cooldown and re-arm, in recognizer.py.

  5. 05 · output

    Gated adapter

    Only the runtime calls it, and only after checking can_emit.

Quick start

Ninety seconds to a dry run.

The dry run opens the preview and records what it would have sent, without moving your pointer. Leave out --dry-run for real output; it still starts paused.

  • Python 3.9 or newer, and a webcam
  • macOS Camera permission for your terminal
  • Accessibility permission for real pointer output
zsh — kinematic 
$ git clone https://github.com/Marvinoponopano/kinematic.git
$ cd kinematic
$ python3 -m pip install -r requirements.txt
$ python3 -m kinematic.main --dry-run
# check camera, screen, MediaPipe and Accessibility
$ python3 -m kinematic.main --diagnose
